Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how CHAZU AGENCY ("we", "us") collects and uses personal information when you visit this website or ask us for a free call. You can reach us at chazuagency@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • What you give us: your full name, business name, email address, mobile number, website address (if any), a description of your needs, your chosen call day and time, and your consent to be contacted.
  • What is collected automatically: technical information such as your browser, device, approximate location, pages viewed and time zone, through cookies and similar tools described below.

3. How you send us a request

When you submit the call request form, your email app opens with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent until you press send in your own email app. We receive the request by email.

4. How we use it

  • To arrange and confirm your free call, and to reply to your messages.
  • To prepare a quote and deliver our services if you become a client.
  • To call, text or message you on the mobile number you provide about your meeting, as you agreed when you ticked the consent box.
  • To understand how the site is used and to improve it.
  • To measure and improve our advertising.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

5. Calls, texts and messages

We contact you by phone, text or messaging app only about your request or meeting, and only after you have agreed. Message and data rates from your provider may apply. You can tell us to stop at any time by replying STOP or by emailing us.

6. Cookies, analytics and advertising

We may use cookies and similar technologies to count visits and see which pages are useful. We may also use advertising pixels, which let us measure how our ads perform and show our ads to people who visited this site. These tools may collect your IP address, device information and browsing activity, and the companies that provide them may process this data under their own policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and limit ad personalization in your device or account ad settings. Blocking cookies does not stop you from using this site.

7. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site, send email, analyze visits, run advertising or take payments, and only as needed for those purposes. We may also disclose information when the law requires it or to protect our rights.

8. How long we keep it

We keep request and client information as long as needed to serve you and meet legal and accounting needs, then delete or anonymize it. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so unless we must keep it by law.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to your information, a correction, deletion, or to object to certain uses, including advertising based on your activity. To use any of these rights, email chazuagency@gmail.com. We will answer within a reasonable time, and we may ask you to confirm your identity first.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or email can be fully secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy: chazuagency@gmail.com.
